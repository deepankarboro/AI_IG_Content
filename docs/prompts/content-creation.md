# ✍️ AI Prompts for Content Creation & Marketing

High-conversion, tested prompt templates for turning technical ideas into engaging posts, threads, newsletters, and YouTube video scripts.

---

### 1. The "Viral Technical Social Hook & Carousel"
> Converts a complex GitHub repo or AI tool into a high-engagement 5-slide carousel / thread.

```text
You are a top 0.1% technical content creator and growth strategist.
Take the following technical tool or concept and convert it into a viral 5-slide carousel / social post.

Structure:
- Slide 1 (Hook): A curiosity-inducing hook addressing a major pain point or expensive software alternative.
- Slide 2 (The Problem): Why traditional tools cost too much time or money.
- Slide 3 (The Solution): Introduce the open-source tool, its key mechanism, and why it feels "illegal to know".
- Slide 4 (Step-by-Step Run): Exact terminal command or quick prompt to get started in under 60 seconds.
- Slide 5 (Call to Action): Save this post, share with teammates, or check GitHub link.

Target Tool / Concept:
<INSERT_TOOL_OR_TOPIC_HERE>
```

---

### 2. The "YouTube Short / Reel 45-Second Script"
> Generates an energetic, high-retention 45-second video script with visual cue markers.

```text
Write a 45-second high-retention video script for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels about:
<TOPIC_OR_REPO>

Guidelines:
- [0:00 - 0:03]: Visual + Verbal Hook (Stop the scroll).
- [0:04 - 0:15]: The "Aha!" moment / Demo showing what it does.
- [0:16 - 0:35]: The exact 1-line command or step to use it.
- [0:36 - 0:45]: Clean outro with CTA.

Format:
Use two columns:
- [Visual / Screen Action]
- [Voiceover Audio]
```
